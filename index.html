GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might wonder.

Do I need yoga experience?

No. The retreat is being designed to welcome both beginners and people with an established practice. The final session levels will be confirmed with the programme.

How much will the retreat cost?

Pricing has not yet been announced. Early-access members will be among the first to receive the confirmed package and pricing details.

Where exactly will it take place?

The retreat is planned for the Freiburg and Black Forest area. The exact venue and travel guidance will be shared once confirmed.

What does joining the waitlist mean?

It is a no-obligation way to hear about dates and booking availability first. No payment is taken and a place is not reserved.

Can dietary or accessibility needs be accommodated?

We intend to make the experience as welcoming as possible. Please share any requirements when booking details are released so we can confirm what the venue can support.
